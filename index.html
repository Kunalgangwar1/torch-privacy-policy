Torch – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Torch is a simple Android app with one purpose: turning your device's flashlight on and off when you tap a button. Your privacy is important to us, and the app is designed to work without collecting or transmitting your data.

Information We Collect

Torch does not collect, store, process, or transmit personal information or any other user data. No account or registration is required to use the app.

Device Permissions

Torch accesses only the device hardware permission necessary to control the flashlight. Although Android may describe this as camera access on some devices or operating system versions, Torch does not take photos, record video, or access stored media.

Data Sharing

Because Torch does not collect any data, it does not sell, rent, disclose, or share information with third parties. No data is sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Torch does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or other third-party services that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Torch does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. Since the app collects no personal data, it does not retain information about children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last Updated” date above will be revised to reflect the effective date of the update.
